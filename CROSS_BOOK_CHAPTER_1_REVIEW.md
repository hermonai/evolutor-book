Date: 6 September 2026. This is an internal author-agent comparison, not an independent evaluation.

| Dimension | DNA Computing | Evolutor | Decision |

Both have nine chapter pages. Their front/back matter differs because Evolutor needs a larger biological/research glossary. The code pages can be skipped without losing the narrative. Figures alone convey the arc, but captions remain necessary to constrain scientific interpretation.

The strongest production lesson is that layout and scientific interpretation interact: a caption cannot repair an arrow that visually implies the wrong process, and a float can interrupt a sentence despite a clean build. The same fixes and production standard are carried into both repositories.
