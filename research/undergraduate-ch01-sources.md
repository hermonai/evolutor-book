# Chapter 1 source register

Access date: 6 September 2026. Sources support facts, not copied artwork. All figures and examples are original; third-party images and full papers are not redistributed.

- [NHGRI Gene](https://www.genome.gov/genetics-glossary/Gene) and [Genome](https://www.genome.gov/genetics-glossary/Genome): complete definitions inspected. Use the functional-RNA-inclusive gene description; genome is not treated as sufficient cellular machinery.
- [NHGRI RNA](https://www.genome.gov/genetics-glossary/Ribonucleic-Acid-RNA) and [Protein](https://www.genome.gov/genetics-glossary/Protein): complete definitions/narration inspected. Used only for introductory roles and protein building units.
- [Alberts et al., An Overview of Gene Control](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK26885/): relevant chapter sections and summary read. Use the distinction between DNA sequence and context-dependent expression, not outdated gene counts or any copied illustration.
- [Python control-flow tutorial](https://docs.python.org/3/tutorial/controlflow.html): official reference for definitions, conditionals and return; local code is independently written and tested on Python 3.13.6.

## Evidence limits

There are no biological measurements, model-training runs, performance comparisons or independent reader studies in this chapter. The source register distinguishes a historical observation from a schematic, an analogy and a proposed research question. Later mechanisms require renewed primary-source review.
