## Primary-source work by domain

DNA origins: Adleman and Lipton, followed by original sticker/splicing/insertion–deletion papers with exact model variants. Molecular foundations: original/authoritative chemistry sources for nearest-neighbor parameters, salt/concentration assumptions, ligase/polymerase substrates and measurement procedures. Molecular programming: Benenson-style automata, Seeman/Winfree self-assembly, strand-displacement and circuit/CRN papers, including experimental limitations. Modern frontier: refresh a source-bounded survey when the chapter is drafted rather than freezing a “latest” claim now.

Evolutor foundations: original recurrent/gated models, S4/S5, selective SSMs, RWKV, attention/Transformer, MoE, developmental encodings and structural learning. Systems: original cache/paging, scheduling, quantization, speculative decoding, kernel and distributed-execution work, plus exact versions of implementation documentation. Runtime/IR/database analogies need both the established system semantics and a precise proposed correspondence.

For every use record citation, claim, accessed portion, assumptions, rights for reused material, independent derivation or reproduction, and limitations. All new illustrations should be original; reproducing a paper's image requires rights review. No external code, copied diagrams or unverified bibliography entries are introduced in this milestone.
