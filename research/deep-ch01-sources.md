# Deep Chapter 1 source and originality review

Access/review date: 7 September 2026. Author-agent review, not independent endorsement. Only the portions named below were used. The bibliography is selective, not a completed field survey. No source figures, code, or long quotations were copied. Graphs, exact programs, arithmetic, sampling derivation, and memory calculations are original teaching constructions with declared assumptions.

## expression

[National Human Genome Research Institute. Gene Expression.](https://www.genome.gov/genetics-glossary/Gene-Expression)

Access and use: Glossary read; expression yields functional RNA/protein; no software-equivalence claim.

## control

[Bruce Alberts et al. An Overview of Gene Control. Molecular Biology of the Cell, 4th edition (2002).](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK26885/)

Access and use: Opening and control-level discussion: shared genomes, differing activity, multiple regulatory stages. Obsolete gene-count estimates excluded.

## development

[Scott F. Gilbert. Principles of Development: Genes and Development. Developmental Biology, 6th edition (2000).](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK10050/)

Access and use: Principles and genomic-equivalence exception read. Uses genotype/phenotype relation only, not dated predictions about cloning.

## environment

[Scott F. Gilbert. Mechanisms for the environmental regulation of gene expression. Birth Defects Research C 72 (2004), 291--299.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15662704/)

Access and use: Indexed abstract via search; direct page challenged. Narrow claim that environmental influence can affect developmental gene expression; no detailed mechanism reconstructed.

## moe

[Noam Shazeer et al. Outrageously Large Neural Networks: The Sparsely-Gated Mixture-of-Experts Layer (2017).](https://arxiv.org/abs/1701.06538)

Access and use: Primary abstract: learned sparse gating selects experts. No benchmark claims repeated.

## attention

[Ashish Vaswani et al. Attention Is All You Need (2017).](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762)

Access and use: Primary HTML introduction and attention equations/shapes read. Conventional KV array budget separately derived under explicit assumptions.

## nas

[Barret Zoph and Quoc V. Le. Neural Architecture Search with Reinforcement Learning (2017).](https://arxiv.org/abs/1611.01578)

Access and use: Primary abstract: model descriptions generated and trained through reinforcement learning. No performance claims repeated.

## synthesis

[Sumit Gulwani, Alex Polozov, and Rishabh Singh. Program Synthesis (2017).](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/program-synthesis/)

Access and use: Author publication summary read; specification-to-program task definition, not full survey review.

## gp

[John R. Koza. Research overview: genetic programming.](https://www.genetic-programming.com/johnkoza.html)

Access and use: Author research overview and indexed description of evolutionary program search; not a proof of Evolutor novelty.

## mamba

[Albert Gu and Tri Dao. Mamba: Linear-Time Sequence Modeling with Selective State Spaces (2023; revised 2024).](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.00752)

Access and use: Primary abstract: input-dependent state-space parameters and selective state propagation. Full semantics/experiments deferred.

## react

[Shunyu Yao et al. ReAct: Synergizing Reasoning and Acting in Language Models (2023).](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.03629)

Access and use: Primary abstract: interleaved reasoning/actions with environment access. No AGI or project capability inferred.
