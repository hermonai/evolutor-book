# Storyboard

1. Adaptation creates a new artifact with explicit lineage rather than mutating the active artifact.
2. Promotion eligibility is conjunctive across required quality, safety and compatibility gates.
3. Canary exposure follows offline evaluation and has explicit rollback thresholds.
4. Promotion changes serving identity, not candidate bytes.
5. Rollback restores a prior validated serving artifact but cannot undo irreversible external effects.
6. Proposal and deployment authority are deliberately separated.
