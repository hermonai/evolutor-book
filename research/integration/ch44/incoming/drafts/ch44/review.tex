\chapter*{Author technical review}
\begin{itemize}
\item Artifact identity binds model/runtime/config/schema/provenance.
\item Liveness and readiness are separated.
\item Draining prevents new admissions and waits for in-flight ownership.
\item Promotion is SLO-gated and rollback is explicit.
\item Telemetry semantics separate traces, metrics and logs and avoid requiring sensitive content.
\item The oracle proves state-machine invariants, not production availability.
\end{itemize}
