\chapter*{Author technical review}
\begin{itemize}
\item Scheduling review: per-iteration token budget is enforced; arrived/not-arrived and completed requests are distinguished; decode priority is explicit rather than universal.
\item Tail review: TTFT, ITL and completion are separately defined; throughput is not used as their surrogate.
\item Precision review: FP16/BF16/INT8 are representation transformations with independent reference comparisons; no hardware speed claim is inferred.
\item Quality review: max error, relative $L_2$ and argmax stability are necessary diagnostics, not sufficient task validation.
\item Boundary review: speculative decoding and optimized attention kernels remain EVOD-38.
\end{itemize}
Independent specialist and render reviews remain open.
