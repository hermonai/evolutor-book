\chapter*{Source notes}
\begin{itemize}
\item Yu et al. (2022), \emph{Orca}, OSDI: iteration-level scheduling addresses multi-iteration autoregressive workloads and dynamic batch membership.
\item Agrawal et al. (2024), \emph{Sarathi-Serve}, OSDI: chunked/stall-free batching highlights the throughput versus time-between-token latency interaction between prefills and decodes.
\item Sheng et al. (2024), \emph{Fairness in Serving Large Language Models}, OSDI: continuous batching also raises client-level fairness questions; fairness is not implied by work conservation.
\item Xiao et al. (2023), \emph{SmoothQuant}, ICML/arXiv:2211.10438: W8A8 post-training quantization and activation-outlier handling illustrate why quantization requires a method plus empirical accuracy evaluation.
\item EVOD-35--36: full/cached causal parity and paged KV ownership are prerequisites for scheduling and low-precision serving.
\end{itemize}
