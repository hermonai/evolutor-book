# Continuation contract

EVOD-23 produced a negative Hermon motif result. EVOD-24 established data/objective
contracts and an independent motif oracle. EVOD-25 prohibited false equivalence.
EVOD-26 established training/inference parity. EVOD-27/28 established optimization,
distributed-state and artifact correctness.

EVOD-29 turns those contracts into comparative evidence:

- task/data/oracle are frozen before model ranking;
- multiple seeds and sources of variation are reported as distributions;
- budgets are vectors, not a single parameter-count slogan;
- capacity-matched and compute-matched comparisons answer different questions;
- interventions/ablations must have explicit causal targets;
- transplant tests move a mechanism into another architecture when feasible;
- benchmark score, systems efficiency and capability claims are separate;
- negative results remain first-class evidence.
