\chapter*{About the examples, sources, and evidence}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{About the examples, sources, and evidence}

This book develops its explanations through small objects that readers can
inspect: predict a result, derive the mechanism, implement it, compare it with
an independent check, and investigate where the assumptions fail.
Sebastian Raschka's author companion overview and chapter map for
\emph{Build a Large Language Model (From Scratch)} informed this general
implementation-first progression:
\url{https://www.sebastianraschka.com/llms-from-scratch/}.
The companion was inspected, not the full commercial text.
The prose, reference implementations, numerical examples, exercises, and
illustrations authored for this edition are original unless explicitly
attributed otherwise. This is a pedagogical acknowledgment, not a claim
to reproduce that book.

An executable calculation establishes a result within its stated model.
Computed values are not experimental measurements, a trained genomic
model, or a measured performance advantage unless the chapter supplies
that evidence explicitly. Chapter bibliographies identify the sources
and sections actually inspected; reading an abstract is not represented
as reviewing a complete paper.

Author technical, editorial, and visual checks are separate from independent
specialist review and observation of real readers. Current review status
is recorded in the edition's release documentation. Chapter-specific
assumptions and scientific limitations remain beside the relevant claims;
this shared note avoids repeating the same editorial disclosure after
every chapter.
