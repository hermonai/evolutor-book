Appendix A plate. Panel A: an eight-base-pair duplex. Upper strand 5'-ACGGTCAT-3' (left to right); lower strand paired antiparallel, written 3'-TGCCAGTA-5' left to right. Half-arrowheads mark 3' ends; grey rungs are base pairs (A-T, G-C). Text: the lower strand read 5' to 3' is ATGACCGT = R(ACGGTCAT); complementing without reversing gives TGCCAGTA, which is the wrong reading direction.
Panel B: coding strand 5'-ATGGCACTG-3' over template 3'-TACCGTGAC-5'. Transcription (RNA polymerase reads the template 3' to 5' and builds RNA 5' to 3') gives mRNA 5'-AUGGCACUG-3', with U in place of T, boxed into three codons. Translation (ribosome reads codons 5' to 3') gives the peptide Met-Ala-Leu (AUG, GCA, CUG). Base letters are printed in every tile so colour is not required. Schematic, not to scale.
