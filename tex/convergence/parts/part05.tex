\PartQuestion{What must a Transformer-based DNA model specify before its genomic claims mean anything?}
Hermon DNA starts from an ordinary pre-norm Transformer over single nucleotides and makes every genomic convention explicit: masks, padding, reverse-complement readouts, base spans, span-level causality, strand embeddings, rotary positions in base units and long-context access graphs. The pilot experiment is reported as it came out: under its small budget, the Hermon reference did not fit the task while a four-wide convolution partly did. Treat this as a lesson, not an embarrassment. Correct algebra and a trained model are different achievements.
