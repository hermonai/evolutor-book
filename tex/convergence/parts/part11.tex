\PartQuestion{What evidence would show that these systems are useful for genomics, persistent agents, continual learning or general capability?}
The application chapters turn capability language into experimental designs. Genomic tasks need leakage-resistant splits, strand-correct labels and calibrated metrics, and they need biological validation when mechanisms are claimed. Persistent agents need event logs, intent-before-dispatch recovery and reconciliation. Continual learning needs a full acquisition--retention matrix. Claims about general intelligence need held-out task families, budget axes and an honest statement of what finite tests cannot prove.
