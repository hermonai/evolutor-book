\PartQuestion{How does a native recurrent state become a correct, owned and batched serving object?}
The DOGMA Engine chapters wrap the restricted Chapter~31 recurrence in a serving lifecycle. The pieces are request identity and terminal arbitration, artifact-bound native state, generational state pools with in-flight pins, checkpoints and independent branches, and request-axis batching with fenced completion. Every optimization is stated as a refinement of a simpler oracle. The engine is a CPU reference: these chapters establish contracts and accounting, not measured throughput.
