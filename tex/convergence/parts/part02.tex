\PartQuestion{What does a sequence model actually compute, learn and remember, and how do we test each part before trusting it?}
These nine chapters build the machine-learning foundations from first principles: objectives and legal information, hand-derived gradients, reproducible training, tokenization, recurrence, state-space scans, attention, the Transformer block and conditional routing. Every mechanism is implemented in small, inspectable PyTorch and checked against an independent oracle, often with a deliberately broken negative control beside it. The genomic examples are explicit data contracts, not claims about biology. Readers who already know deep learning should still read Chapters~2, 5 and 7: target alignment, token reach and associative scans are reused throughout the book.
