\chapter*{About this development edition}
This cumulative edition contains Chapters 1 and 2, not a completed book. Chapter 1's source and previously published PDF are preserved. The new chapter adds mechanisms, original vector figures, exact mathematical examples, executable references, exercises and worked solutions.

The intended standard is depth with straightforward explanation, without an artificial educational-level ceiling. Figures have editable SVG sources and semantic TXT companions. The repository also includes static state-changing keyframes, not exported moving media.

No new laboratory experiment, trained DOGMA or Hermon DNA result, GPU training run or inference-performance benchmark is reported. Computed teaching examples are explicitly distinguished from historical evidence and measured scientific results.

Review is author-agent source, mathematical, skeptical and rendered-page review, not independent specialist certification. Independent review and observation of real readers remain open. This PDF is not tagged and is not claimed to satisfy PDF/UA.
