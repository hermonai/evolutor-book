\chapter*{About this prototype}
This is the first chapter of the canonical deep edition, not a completed book. The earlier introductory experiment is frozen; it may later inform a derivative, but it is not maintained chapter by chapter.

The chapter assumes technical maturity in programming and elementary mathematics. Domain-specific ideas are explained through mechanisms, original diagrams, formal definitions and worked examples. Useful abstraction is part of the explanation, not something to avoid.

All figures are original editable vectors with semantic TXT companions. Static keyframes are included in the repository; they are not exported animations. Exact numerical examples come from tested Python. No new laboratory experiment, trained DNA-native model or production inference engine is reported.

Review is internal author-agent scientific, mathematical, clarity, citation and rendered-page review. Independent specialist review and observation of real readers remain open. This PDF is not tagged and is not claimed to meet PDF/UA.
