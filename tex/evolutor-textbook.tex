\chapter*{How to read this revision}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{How to read this revision}
Begin with the concrete example, predict its behavior, then check the derivation
and executable reference. Figures show mechanisms and evidence boundaries,
not merely names connected by arrows. Worked solutions distinguish a closed
calculation from an open scientific investigation.

Chapters 1 and 2 retain their accepted authored LaTeX without changing their
reviewed files. Chapter 3 is now an editable LaTeX manuscript, with its original
vector figures, worked solutions and a new native TikZ bridge from the preceding
chapter. The build does not export prose from Markdown. Research notes and
historical Markdown reviews remain available as evidence, not authoring inputs.

This is a first series-style revision, not a claim of independent specialist
review or a complete book. Its pedagogical structure is original: concrete
problem, explicit assumptions, derivation, implementation, counterexample,
evidence and next question. No biological result, trained-model capability or
industrial performance claim is established merely by a teaching diagram.
