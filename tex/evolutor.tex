\chapter*{Edition and rights}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Edition and rights}
This development manuscript begins a new intellectual edition while preserving
the earlier manuscript, code and correction history in Git and a historical
directory. No old chapter is included in this build.

Author attribution is unchanged. No new copyright holder, open-source license
or permission to redistribute third-party material is asserted. Publication
metadata will be finalized with the rights holder before a final edition.

AI assistance is a research and authoring process, not evidence for a scientific
claim. External sources, mathematical reasoning, executable checks and measured
experiments have separate evidentiary roles.

The accompanying repository contains the research map, source-access records,
candidate contents, audit decisions and canonical text graphs.
