# Canonical edition strategy

Effective 7 September 2026, both repositories advance only astra-deep-rewrite. The target is full scientific/mathematical/engineering depth with direct explanations, original mechanism figures, worked examples and executable reference semantics. Useful abstraction is welcome. Basic programming and mathematics are assumed; domain-specific ideas are taught.

astra-undergraduate-rewrite is frozen as a pedagogical archive. Reuse design lessons selectively; do not synchronize prose, illustrations, code, PDFs or reviews chapter by chapter. A later foundation edition may be derived from mature deep chapters after an explicit request. Main, astra-rewrite, tags, historical snapshots and previous PDFs remain preserved.

The current milestone adds Chapter 2 to the preserved Chapter 1 in each book. The 32-chapter DNA and 52-chapter Evolutor architectures remain intact. New cumulative entry points and PDF filenames preserve the previous publications byte-for-byte. Chapter 3 onward remains planned. No trained model, new laboratory experiment, optimized engine or AGI capability is delivered.

DOGMA = non-Transformer DNA-native model + DOGMA Engine; Hermon DNA = Transformer-based DNA model + Hermon DNA Engine; Evolutor = broader theory/research/compiler/runtime and orchestration above both. A model specifies semantics; an engine implements them; orchestration composes capabilities. None is proved by its name.

The shared Book I contract marks DNAD-01 and DNAD-02 as prototype-available. Later imports remain planned, not demonstrated knowledge. Internal review and exercises do not constitute independent reader validation. Ordinary CS baselines and negative/equivalence results remain part of the research.
