\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Sources and evidence}
Access checked 27 September 2026. Mamba arXiv v2: Sections 3.1--3.3 inspected
for input-dependent coefficients and execution dependencies. Evo 2: publisher
abstract and architecture/training section inspected, particularly the mixture
of convolutional operators and attention. Neither paper's reported benchmark
numbers are used as evidence for DOGMA. Full experimental reproduction,
training-data auditing and independent hardware benchmarking were not performed.

The proposed pair-table operator, worked counterexamples, implementation,
derivations and figures are original teaching artifacts. Convexity, logistic
derivatives and affine composition are standard mathematics, not novelty claims.
The small functional ablations are not genomic validation or production-engine
results. The shared book front matter owns the general pedagogical acknowledgment;
it is not repeated here. Independent specialist and reader review remain open.
