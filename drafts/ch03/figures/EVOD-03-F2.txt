EVOD-03-F2: Reverse accumulation is local accounting

QUESTION
How do all parameters receive gradients?

OBJECTS
Loss adjoint
logit error
projection gradients
tanh derivative
bias reduction

RELATION / MECHANISM
Seed scalar loss with one and propagate vector-Jacobian products

INFERENCE
Reused values receive the sum of all downstream contributions.

BOUNDARY
A computation DAG is not necessarily the model's information-flow guarantee.

SOURCE
