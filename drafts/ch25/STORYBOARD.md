# EVOD-25 figure storyboard

1. Envelope: fork a shared scientific contract into native DOGMA recurrence and
Hermon attention paths, joining only at declared scoring and resource accounting.
2. Contracts: exact reverse complement, declared groups and curated links form
one transitive dependency component, even without a direct endpoint edge.
3. Budget: compare actual parameter inventories, full DOGMA state and Hermon's
lower-triangular causal graph; keep static quantities separate from measurements.
4. Seeds: keep paired correctness attached to records and show four correlated
clusters. Row and cluster resampling have the same observed mean but different
intervals; training randomness is a separate experimental axis.
5. Intervention: ablation, substitution and transplant preserve the task envelope
while answering different causal questions.
6. Diagnosis: label/legality audit, tiny-set fit, held-out baselines, bounded
interpretation and explicit intervention retain negative results without hype.
