Figure 32.6 — Capacity, canaries and reuse.
Capacity equals free + owned + retiring slots. Pins count unsettled work.
Deterministic canary histories compare the pool with an independent owner ledger,
including cancellation and generation reuse. Payload size 8*C*D counts logical
float64 entries, not Python overhead or measured GPU memory. Serialized tests
do not certify concurrent hardware safety.
