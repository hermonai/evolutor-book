\chapter*{Sources and further reading}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Sources and further reading}
Access checked 21 September 2026. Sections 2.1--2.2 of the Switch paper,
including its routing, capacity and auxiliary-loss equations, were inspected
in the JMLR PDF. The PyTorch 2.10 argmax description was inspected for tie
behavior. The local CPU float64 reference reuses the Chapter 9 normalization
and attention implementation, which is included in the reviewed source closure.

The deterministic policy, six-token traces, negative controls, gradient
derivations, and cost comparisons are teaching artifacts. They do not
reproduce Switch's distributed implementation, training experiments or
performance claims.
