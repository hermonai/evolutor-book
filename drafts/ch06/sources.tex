\section*{Teaching and evidence boundaries}
All API sources were inspected on 19 September 2026.
The author companion to Sebastian Raschka's
\emph{Build a Large Language Model (From Scratch)} informed the general
read--derive--implement--test progression:
\url{https://www.sebastianraschka.com/llms-from-scratch/}.
Only the companion overview and chapter map were inspected, not the full
commercial text. Prose, code, examples, and vector figures are original.

Synthetic retention curves and assigned cell weights do not establish a
trained DNA model, task performance, or biological generalization. Author
review remains distinct from independent specialist and reader review.
