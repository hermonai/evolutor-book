\bibitem{lstm-original}
Sepp Hochreiter and Jürgen Schmidhuber (1997). Long Short-Term Memory.
\emph{Neural Computation} 9(8), 1735--1780.
\url{https://www.bioinf.jku.at/publications/older/2604.pdf}.
Introduction and early gradient-transport discussion inspected on
19 September 2026. Cited for motivation, not as the source of every feature
in a modern forget-gate cell. No reported experimental performance is reproduced.

\bibitem{lstm-api}
PyTorch 2.10 documentation. \texttt{LSTMCell}.
\url{https://docs.pytorch.org/docs/2.10/generated/torch.nn.LSTMCell.html}.
Equations, input/state shapes, and parameter layout inspected.
Our implementation follows the documented modern unprojected, no-peephole cell.

\bibitem{gru-api}
PyTorch 2.10 documentation. \texttt{GRUCell}.
\url{https://docs.pytorch.org/docs/2.10/generated/torch.nn.GRUCell.html}.
Gate equations, state convention, and packed weight/bias shapes inspected.
Local parity checks use PyTorch 2.10.0, CPU float64, and assigned weights.

\bibitem{gru-variant}
PyTorch 2.10 documentation. \texttt{GRU}, implementation note.
\url{https://docs.pytorch.org/docs/2.10/generated/torch.nn.GRU.html}.
The reset-before versus reset-after distinction was inspected explicitly.
Our matrix counterexample and diagrams are original.
\end{thebibliography}
