\chapter{Failures, open problems and reproducible synthesis}
\label{evod52:chapter}
A final ledger contains one hypothesis, one unsuccessful comparison and one
preserved counterexample. Has the project failed, or has it learned something?
The useful answer names what the counterexample rules out, which implementation
still behaves correctly, and what experiment comes next. A research book should
end with fewer vague claims than it began with.

This chapter joins the failure taxonomy, claim/experiment/theorem ledgers and
post-book agenda to the repository's actual evidence. All 52 chapters are now
assembled as a complete manuscript candidate with executable references.
That is not publication acceptance, a deployed production Evolutor runtime,
a trained genomic result or a demonstration of AGI.

\section{What is actually implemented, and what is still staged?}
\index{model semantics}\index{execution systems}\index{capability science}
\index{research synthesis!evidence boundaries}
Separate three research layers. Model semantics define functions and state
transitions. Systems engineering preserves those functions while changing
storage, scheduling and execution. Capability science tests behavior on held-out
tasks. A correct kernel does not prove a learning hypothesis; a predictive gain
does not validate a biological analogy.

\section{Evidence matrices should preserve the closest competitor}
\index{baseline controls}\index{budget accounting}
For DOGMA, the central question is quality--memory--latency tradeoff against
strong recurrent/SSM and other appropriate baselines. Markov/MLP/CNN controls
matter when local statistics or motifs can solve the task. Transformer and
long-context controls matter when addressable history is the claimed advantage.
Non-Transformer identity is an architectural fact, not evidence of superiority.

For Hermon DNA, hold the Transformer backbone, training opportunity and task
contract fixed while ablating the proposed genomic mechanism. Compare
strand-aware transformations against augmentation and shared two-pass pooling;
compare coordinate policies at the same legal information boundary. A faster
attention kernel can improve serving cost while leaving learned quality
unchanged. Charge extra data, context and tuning rather than crediting those
gains to the biological inductive bias.

For Evolutor, evaluate persistent workflow completion, state recovery,
provenance, authorization, governance and resource accounting. Measure human
intervention as part of the workload. A policy gate can correctly deny an action
that would have increased raw completion rate. Task success and policy success
need distinct denominators and a declared joint admissibility rule.

Every comparison records parameters, training examples/tokens, optimizer and
tuning budget, state/context budget, precision, hardware time, seeds and service
constraints, under the earlier shared evidence contracts~\cite{evod52:contracts}.
Modeled operation counts, synthetic scores, CPU timings and actual
accelerator measurements are different evidence. Absence of a measured hardware
column should appear as unmeasured, never as zero cost.

\section{Turn a symptom into a durable failure artifact}
\index{failure taxonomy}\index{counterexample}\index{negative evidence}
The incoming taxonomy includes semantic correctness, numerical instability,
causality/target leakage, baseline and optimization regressions, scaling,
contamination, reproducibility, security/policy, service SLOs, biological
interpretation and capability overclaim. These categories may overlap. A
noncausal feature can cause both an implausible loss and a false capability claim.
Store a primary category plus the causal explanation instead of forcing every
failure into exactly one box.

Reduce a failing run to the smallest input that preserves the discrepancy.
Keep the original run too: minimization can remove conditions relevant to
deployment frequency. Record expected behavior, observed behavior, artifact,
environment, command, data identity and exact comparison rule. A later fix adds
a resolution record and a regression test; it does not delete the original
counterexample.

\Listing{Ledger.add_failure}
The new failure check binds claim and experiment to the same identity. The
incoming reference checked that both existed but allowed a failure about one
claim to name another claim's experiment. This relational error could make
the wrong research direction appear disproved or resolved. The local tests
include that mismatch as a negative control.

Known audit targets include overlapping-window and homology leakage, target
bases embedded in tokens, incompatible RC labels, cross-record state carry,
stale generations, silent fallback, invalid perplexity denominators, external
tool replay and tuned-on final benchmarks. These are failure hypotheses to
test continually, not a claim that every current system exhibits them.

\section{Random-data sanity is an expectation with assumptions}
\index{cross entropy!uniform target}\index{Jensen inequality}
Let the target $Y$ be uniformly distributed over $V$ classes and independent
of the predictor's permitted context. Conditional on that context, the
predictor supplies probabilities $q_1,\ldots,q_V>0$ summing to one.
The expected next-target cross entropy in nats is
\begin{equation}\label{evod52:entropy}
\mathbb E[-\log q_Y]= -\frac1V\sum_{i=1}^V\log q_i
=\log V+D_{\mathrm{KL}}(U\|q)\ge\log V.
\end{equation}
Equivalently, Jensen's inequality for convex $-\log$ gives
$-V^{-1}\sum_i\log q_i\ge-\log(V^{-1}\sum_iq_i)=\log V$.
Equality holds for uniform $q$. Zero probability for a possible target gives
infinite expected loss. This is a population statement under independence.

For $V=4$, uniform prediction gives $\log4\approx1.38629$ nats.
For assigned $q=(.7,.1,.1,.1)$ the expected loss is larger, but one sampled
A has loss $-\log .7\approx.356675$, below $\log4$. Thus a finite empirical
average below the expectation is not logically impossible. Report variation,
sample size, class distribution and dependence before declaring leakage.
Sustained implausible gains warrant audits of target alignment, overlapping
tokens, padding, masks and loss denominators. A shuffled-label control must
also preserve the intended sampling structure; shuffling within leaked groups
can leave an unintended shortcut.

\section{Causality tests need a changing negative control}
\index{causality!suffix intervention}\index{negative control}
For a causal predictor, changing suffix $x_{p:}$ while retaining prefix
$x_{:p}$ must not change logits strictly before $p$. Freeze model mode and
numerical conditions. A test comparing an output tensor with itself proves
nothing; the changed suffix must actually be different, and at least one
permitted later output should change in a suitable positive control.

The local test calls Chapter 35's full oracle, which calls the original
Chapter 23 causal path. It compares ACGT and ACAA: the first two logits are
equal and later logits differ on the assigned model. This checks a real
implemented interface without substituting a detached attention toy. It does
not prove every possible model setting by finite sampling. The architectural
causal proof still depends on legal feature construction and layer masks.
An offline reverse-complement average is useful for an invariant classification
task but cannot silently feed future sequence into a next-base predictor.

\section{A ledger distinguishes plans, observations and conclusions}
\index{claim ledger}\index{experiment ledger}\index{theorem ledger}\index{failure resolution}
Each claim has exact wording, scope, evidence stage and falsifier. Each
experiment has a hypothesis link, baseline, budget, metric, threshold,
data/artifact identity, seeds, sampling unit and an outcome. A planned run has
no observed confidence bound. A completed failed run is evidence against the
criterion, not an implementation milestone that unlocks a benchmark claim.

\Listing{Ledger.promote}
The reference imports the owned Chapter 51 evidence contract. It rejects
empirical promotion while a linked counterexample remains unresolved. A
resolution must cite a distinct supporting experiment; the failure remains in
the capsule. This policy deliberately favors explicit treatment of negative
evidence. It does not adjudicate scientific disputes: an experiment labeled
successful may still be invalid, and an apparent resolution requires human
mechanistic/statistical review.

\section{Reproducibility is a dependency closure, not one checksum}
\index{artifact capsule}\index{dependency closure}\index{content digest}
\index{reproducibility!capsule}
A result capsule needs source revision and dirty-tree identity; data,
tokenizer/model identities and licenses; complete configuration and seeds;
compiler, runtime, library, driver and hardware versions; precision; exact
commands; raw measurements/checkpoints/logs; analysis scripts; tolerances;
known failures and exclusions. A seed alone fixes none of the missing inputs.
Private or licensed data may need controlled access rather than publication,
but the access limitation must be stated.

\Listing{Ledger.capsule}
The ledger capsule canonically sorts records, serializes their exact content
and hashes it. Record insertion order cannot change its identity. Changing a
failure or result changes the digest. Byte integrity and scientific validity
remain distinct: a reproducible error is still an error, but is easier to
investigate than an undocumented one.

The separate file manifest hashes eight actual reference sources: Chapters
23, 31, 35 and 48--52~\cite{evod52:local}. This is a deliberately bounded code closure used by
this batch's checks. It is not a whole-book capsule or an environment lock.
The local review records additionally bind each chapter's authored LaTeX,
figures, tests and resulting PDF. Their claims are local author review with
\texttt{accepted=false} and \texttt{independent\_review=false}.

\Listing{verify_files}
The manifest verifier reports missing and changed files. A separate envelope
hash detects changes to its listed mapping relative to the trusted envelope.
Canonical relative paths and exclusion of symlinks narrow its input boundary;
this trusted local reader is not a hardened hostile-filesystem service. A
concurrent file replacement can invalidate assumptions between checks and use.

\Listing{dependency_order}
The declared dependency graph must name every referenced artifact and contain
no cycle. Depth-first validation marks active ancestors; re-entering one detects
a cycle. After all dependencies of a node are completed, append that node.
By induction, every dependency precedes its consumer in the returned order.
This is a proof about the supplied graph. The routine does not infer imports,
external datasets or dynamic dependencies, so completeness remains an explicit
closure audit.

\section{Open problems require disconfirming experiments}
\subsection{State, regulation and structural learning}
Which information should remain exactly addressable, and which can be
compressed without damaging the task? Compare retention/retrieval distortion
against state bytes and measured latency, including adversarial collisions
and delayed-use tasks. A fixed-size vector is bounded storage, not a guarantee
of sufficient memory.

Does a proposed regulation mechanism improve quality or efficiency beyond
ordinary gating, routing or conditional computation? Name the nearest existing
mechanism, freeze opportunity, and ablate the claimed difference. If gains
disappear under a matched gate control, revise the explanation. For structural
learning, count all proposals, invalid candidates and tuning; test bounded
complexity, inheritance compatibility and regression. Search over structures
is distinct from gradient learning and from deployment authorization.

\subsection{Memory, continual learning and genomic inductive bias}
A rate--distortion--retrievability study can vary exact records, lossy summaries,
native state and parameters under fixed task information. Measure errors caused
by compression separately from retrieval failure and misuse. Include provenance
and deletion constraints, which an unconstrained accuracy objective can ignore.

Continual learning should report acquisition, peak forgetting, signed transfer,
calibration, training cost and replay storage under declared data access. Test
novel skills and poisoning resistance without assuming that a growing memory
store is learning. Genomic inductive-bias tests need coordinate-correct variant
pairs, strand-label actions, homology-aware splits, matched GC controls, task
trained baselines and independent biological follow-up where mechanistic claims
are made. Better synthetic motif arithmetic is not a wet-lab result.

\subsection{World models, agents, self-improvement and open-endedness}
Persistent agents should be tested under controlled stale observations,
ambiguous effects, tool failures and long-horizon changes. Ablate native state,
retrieval and external simulators separately to identify their contribution.
Record human intervention and reconciliation costs. A service that merely
continues running has not thereby solved a longer task.

For self-improvement, preregister held-out criteria and matched search controls
across repeated generations; charge the evaluator and failed proposals too.
For open-endedness, define useful niches and sustained novelty before scoring
new skills. For AGI, retain the unresolved breadth/depth/acquisition questions
from Chapter 51. No finite book artifact closes them. Safety, authority and
human control require their own evidence rather than being assumed from
capability scores.

\section{A staged program and a separate release decision}
\index{integration gate}\index{independent review}\index{publication authorization}
Phase A freezes tasks, baselines and datasets. Phase B tests DOGMA/Hermon scaling
and mechanism ablations. Phase C studies memory and continual adaptation.
Phase D tests persistent interactive agents. Phase E seeks independent
replication and adversarial evaluation. Each phase has a stopping rule: failure
to meet a predeclared effect, budget or policy constraint can end the hypothesis.
Phases are research dependencies, not promised successes or deadlines.

Whole-book integration reconciles source adoption, stable cross-chapter

distinct release gates. The incoming package's completed-spine flag is not
adopted as final-book acceptance. No stage in this chapter authorizes publishing.

\section{Selective glossary}
\begin{description}
\item[Counterexample.] A concrete input and expected/observed discrepancy that
contradicts a stated property under its assumptions.
\item[Resolution record.] New evidence addressing a failure while preserving its
original identity and symptom.
\item[Capsule.] Bound artifacts, environment, commands, raw observations and
analysis needed to reconstruct a scoped result.
\item[Dependency closure.] The complete declared set of inputs and their
relationships; validating a declared graph does not infer hidden dependencies.
\item[Acceptance gate.] A review or authorization decision with its own scope,
separate from passing local tests or producing a PDF.
\end{description}

\section{Final exercises and worked solutions}
\begin{enumerate}
\item A chapter test passes. Which layer is evidenced? \textbf{Answer:}
its stated local semantics, not every capability motivated by the chapter.
\item Is Chapter 31 full DOGMA? \textbf{Answer:} no; it is the actual restricted
input-gated diagonal teaching recurrence used by these engine checks.
\item Does Hermon cache parity establish genomic usefulness? \textbf{Answer:}
no; it preserves a function whose task quality requires separate evaluation.
\item Candidate beats a smaller baseline. Architecture gain? \textbf{Answer:}
not without capacity/opportunity controls appropriate to the claim.
\item Name three failure categories for suspicious loss. \textbf{Answer:}
target leakage, invalid loss denominator and distribution mismatch; finite
sample variation is another explanation to quantify.
\item Derive the expected uniform-target bound. \textbf{Answer:} apply Jensen
to $-\log$ over the normalized probabilities, obtaining $\log V$.
\item Can one random target beat that value empirically? \textbf{Answer:}
yes; a chance A under probability $.7$ has loss $.356675<\log4$.
\item Why change the suffix in a causality test? \textbf{Answer:} identical
inputs or accidentally ignored suffixes can make a test pass vacuously.
\item What does the later-output control add? \textbf{Answer:} evidence that
the assigned perturbation actually changes a permitted part of the computation.
\item Is a planned experiment sufficient for empirical promotion? \textbf{Answer:}
no; it contains no observed successful result or justified confidence bound.
\item Can a failure cite another claim's experiment? \textbf{Answer:} no;
the relational check rejects that inconsistent link.
\item Does resolution delete the failure? \textbf{Answer:} no; it adds a
distinct supporting reference while retaining the counterexample.
\item A digest changed after editing source. \textbf{Answer:} the artifact
identity changed; the old review cannot certify the new bytes.
\item Does a manifest prove dependency completeness? \textbf{Answer:} no;
it verifies named files, while hidden/dynamic dependencies require audit.
\item Prove dependency-before-consumer order. \textbf{Answer:} each node is
appended only after recursively completing all named dependencies; active-stack
re-entry rejects cycles that would violate this ordering.
\item Does a seed form a complete capsule? \textbf{Answer:} no; data, code,
configuration, environment, analysis and failures remain necessary.
\item Design an independent reproduction. \textbf{Rubric:} verified inputs,
matching scoped contract, separate evaluator, raw results, declared numerical
tolerances, failure preservation and externally checked independence.
\item Design a state-memory study. \textbf{Rubric:} fixed information/budget,
exact and compressed controls, delayed retrieval, collision/shift tests,
quality--latency--memory curves, provenance and deletion costs.
\item What remains after manuscript integration? \textbf{Answer:} independent
scientific and reader review, full editorial and accessibility checks, and
explicitly unimplemented production interfaces and empirical studies. A passing
reference adapter does not close those distinct gates.
\item What is the defensible final synthesis? \textbf{Answer:} a collection
of precise implementations, premise-bound proofs and falsifiable research
contracts whose broader empirical claims still require evidence.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Final synthesis}
DOGMA asks whether DNA-native computational organization can provide useful
non-Transformer alternatives. Hermon DNA asks how genomic structure and
Transformer semantics can be combined and served. Evolutor asks how heterogeneous
models, memory, tools, learning and governance can form a persistent research
system. Their strongest future claims must be earned by experiments that can
also reject them. Preserve failures, bind conclusions to exact artifacts, and
keep implementation, integration, independent review and publication separate.
