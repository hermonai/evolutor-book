\section*{Biological and modeling boundaries}
The strand convention follows the explicitly derived orientation rules
in DNA Computing Chapter 5. The tokenizer here accepts canonical uppercase
DNA only and is not an ambiguity-aware biological model. Uniform-base
entropy calculations are hypothetical baselines. Assigned embedding vectors
are not trained representations; resource counts exclude optimizer state
and are not measured runtime benchmarks.
