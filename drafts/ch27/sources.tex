\chapter*{Evidence and source boundaries}
The weighted reduction, manual derivative and Adam oracles, CPU stochastic-replay
counterexample, and equal-size-chain inventory are original teaching artifacts.
The reference uses PyTorch 2.10.0 and Float64; it does not reproduce external
hardware benchmarks or certify mixed-precision training. Paper inspection covers
version-specific author abstracts and metadata, not full experimental sections.
PyTorch inspection covers the 2.10 checkpoint API's selected execution, randomness
and determinism notes. Current documentation for another version is not silently
substituted for the tested runtime.
