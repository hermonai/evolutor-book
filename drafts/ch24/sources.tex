\section*{Sources and evidence}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Sources and evidence}
Primary access checks distinguish established mechanisms from local software
evidence. DNABERT's author implementation README and DNABERT-2's versioned
abstract provide tokenization context. Caduceus HTML Sections 3.2, 4.1 and
Appendix B provide RC architecture context. RoFormer HTML Section 3.2 and its
pair-rotation equations support the relative-position derivation. Longformer's
versioned abstract establishes local/global attention as prior art.
No cited training experiment was reproduced. The small strand and ragged-attention
references are original teaching constructions, not reproductions of those models.
Independent scientific and reader review remain open.
