Ownership and ABA reuse. State identifier S first has allocation generation1 and owner tuple tenantA/requestA/modelA. After physical quiescence and release it may receive generation2 for tenantB/requestB/modelB. A completion ticket carrying generation1 must not affect generation2 even though its string state ID is S. Logical ownership and pinned identity differ from device residency; generations are correctness fences, not authentication capabilities.
