\section*{Sources and evidence}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Sources and evidence}
The pair-addressed state operator, examples, proofs, implementation and plates
are original reference artifacts. DOGMA is a proposed research direction in
this chapter; no trained model, genomic capability or throughput result is
asserted. Reverse-complement symmetry and group averaging are mathematical
properties, not newly claimed inventions.
Caduceus v1's architectural discussion, particularly Sections 3.1--3.2, was
inspected on 25 September 2026 to distinguish bidirectionality from
reverse-complement equivariance. Its reported performance is not used as
evidence for this reference operator. No source artwork or prose was copied.
