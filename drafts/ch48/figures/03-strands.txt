Forward AACGTT is read left to right 5-prime to 3-prime. Its paired lower strand TTGCAA runs 3-prime to 5-prime left to right; reading from the lower 5-prime end yields AACGTT. The example is duplex-palindromic but its ACG motif and CGT partner are distinct. Forward C-to-A at zero-based index 2 corresponds to G-to-T at RC index 3. Rails are backbone continuity; dashed rungs are base pairing.
