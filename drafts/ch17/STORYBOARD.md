## Figure 17.1: Why an active trace is not a causal explanation

- Asset: 01-causal.tex, with semantic TXT companion.

- Change/invariant and caption claim: Node replacement breaks its incoming mechanism, not its downstream edges.

## Figure 17.3: How coalition credit assigns interaction

- Asset: 03-shapley.tex, with semantic TXT companion.
- Objects and arrow meaning: Exact coalition values and permutation marginals.
- Change/invariant and caption claim: Credit sums to baseline-relative output by telescoping.

## Figure 17.4: Credit propagates through retained state

- Asset: 04-time.tex, with semantic TXT companion.
- Objects and arrow meaning: Unrolled scalar recurrence with intervention at a chosen time.
- Change/invariant and caption claim: Later state carries the decayed effect of one earlier input.
- Scientific risk to audit: Changing a recorded value without recomputing descendants is not an intervention.

## Figure 17.5: A test must survive a held-out counterexample
