\renewcommand{\bibname}{Sources and reading notes}
\begin{thebibliography}{9}
\bibitem{blelloch} Guy E. Blelloch.
\emph{Prefix Sums and Their Applications}, author-hosted chapter.
\url{https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~guyb/papers/Ble93.pdf}.
Definitions, parallel implementation discussion, and first-order recurrence
reduction in Sections 1.1, 1.2, and 1.4 inspected on 19 September 2026.
Our numerical pairs, recursive implementation, diagrams, and tests are original.

\bibitem{mamba} Albert Gu and Tri Dao (2024 version).
Mamba: Linear-Time Sequence Modeling with Selective State Spaces.
\url{https://arxiv.org/html/2312.00752v2}.
Sections 2 and 3 inspected on 19 September 2026: discretization, LTI
recurrence/convolution, input selection, and the scan execution rationale.
No reported accuracy, throughput, or GPU-memory benchmark is reproduced.
Our reference is not the paper's full architecture or fused kernel.

\bibitem{s4} Albert Gu, Karan Goel, and Christopher R\'e.
Efficiently Modeling Long Sequences with Structured State Spaces.
\url{https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.00396}.
Abstract and bibliographic record inspected on 20 September 2026.
Used only for the high-level S4 identity and structured-parameterization
distinction, not a full technical reconstruction.

\bibitem{s5} Jimmy T. H. Smith, Andrew Warrington, and Scott W. Linderman.
Simplified State Space Layers for Sequence Modeling.
\url{https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.04933}.
Abstract and bibliographic record inspected on 20 September 2026.
Used for the multi-input, multi-output layer and parallel-scan distinction.
No S5 implementation or benchmark replication is claimed.

\bibitem{rwkv} Bo Peng and collaborators.
RWKV: Reinventing RNNs for the Transformer Era.
\url{https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.13048}.
Original-paper record, abstract, and Section 3.1's architecture description
inspected on 20 September 2026, including
\url{https://arxiv.org/html/2305.13048v2}.
Used for architectural orientation, not claims about later RWKV versions.
\end{thebibliography}
