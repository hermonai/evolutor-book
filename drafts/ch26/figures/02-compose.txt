SEMANTIC GRAPH — Chronological affine composition

Purpose: Explain the mechanism and its scope; companion to the editable TikZ plate.
Notation: prose nodes and typed relationships, not ASCII art.

NODES
N0: Left earlier map (a_L,b_L).
N1: Right later map (a_R,b_R).
N2: Combined scale A=a_R*a_L.
N3: Combined translation B=a_R*b_L+b_R.

RELATIONSHIPS
R0: h -> left -> right: right scales both left contributions.
R1: Associativity permits regrouping with chronological order preserved.
R2: (2,1) then (3,4) yields(6,7); reverse yields(6,9).

READING AND LIMITS
- Not commutative.
- Exact-real associativity does not imply exact floating-point associativity.
- Elementwise cost differs from general matrix-affine composition.

Visual encoding: navy/blue denotes state and bound evidence; green denotes valid flow or independent unit; red denotes failure or limitation. Text and arrow roles preserve meaning in grayscale.
