\chapter*{Evidence and source boundaries}
The affine derivations, reference code, reset/padding contract, independent gradient
oracles and numerical counterexamples are original teaching artifacts. They do not
establish full-DOGMA scan eligibility, Hermon KV parity or hardware performance.
The inspected Blelloch source is the chapter PDF associated with the author's
1993 publication listing, not a claimed reading of the separate 1990 technical report.
Mamba inspection covers selected mechanism sections; S5 inspection covers its
author abstract and metadata. No external training or benchmark result is reproduced.
