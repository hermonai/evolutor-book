\emph{The Algorithmic Beauty of Plants}, printed pp.~1--5, was downloaded and
read for rewriting and D0L-system timing semantics. Later plant models and
the remainder of the book were not inspected for this chapter.

are original teaching artifacts. No plant artwork, architecture-search results
or biological developmental predictions are reproduced.
