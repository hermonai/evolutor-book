QUESTION: What would make an Evolutor claim worth believing?

RELATION: explanatory reading order, not an unlabelled physical cause

STEP 2: Specify an implementable mechanism

STEP 1 → STEP 2 → STEP 3 → STEP 4 → STEP 5
