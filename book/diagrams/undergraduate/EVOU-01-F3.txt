FIGURE EVOU-01-F3: Same DNA, different expression
QUESTION: Must the DNA change whenever cellular activity changes?
OBJECTS: two schematic cell contexts; same DNA gene region; different RNA-production labels
RELATION: explanatory reading order, not an unlabelled physical cause
STEP 1: Hold the shown DNA region fixed
STEP 2: Compare two cellular contexts
STEP 3: Observe possible lower and higher production
STEP 1 → STEP 2 → STEP 3
CAPTION: Gene expression can differ without changing the DNA sequence; cellular machinery is required.
LIMIT: Not measured data, binary gene switches, universal equality of every cell genome, or DNA acting alone.
EVIDENCE: Alberts et al., An Overview of Gene Control; NHGRI gene and genome.
