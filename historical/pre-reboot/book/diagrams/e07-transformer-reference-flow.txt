PARITY OBLIGATION
  full causal logits at position t ⇒ incremental logits after prefill/decode at t,
  within declared tolerance and with identical masking/position semantics.

READING NOTES
1. The reference may recompute prefixes before a KV optimization exists; semantics come first.
2. KV memory grows with processed context in the ordinary design and must be measured by layer and dtype.
3. Causal masking prevents direct future access; intervention tests still check the implementation.
