QUALIFICATION GATE
  architecture family alone ⊣ GCS instantiation.
  Required: persistent typed genome, explicit regulation/expression semantics, trace contract,
  and verified structural-update rules—or a declared narrower subset.

READING NOTES
1. Not every Transformer or recurrent model is a GCS.
2. A GCS can be non-neural; the umbrella is defined by computational roles and semantics.
3. The differentiable and molecular branches remain research proposals until their bridges are specified and tested.
