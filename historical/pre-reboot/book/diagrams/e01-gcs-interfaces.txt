E01 — GENOMIC COMPUTATION SYSTEM: COMPONENTS AND INTERFACES
Scope: decomposes the GCS umbrella into persistent structure, contextual selection, execution, and verified change.

LEGEND
  ╔═╗ persistent boundary   ┌─┐ transient artifact   → data/state flow
   ↺ feedback/state update  ⇒ verified transition   ⊣ rejected candidate

READING NOTES
1. The genome persists across contexts; the expression plan is derived and transient.
2. The verifier is independent of the proposal mechanism. A proposed edit has no authority by itself.
3. The trace records executed causes, but a trace is not automatically a causal proof or learning guarantee.
