SEPARATE ARCHITECTURE DESCRIPTOR
  lineage name · family · architecture version · attention · recurrence · state model ·
  training semantics · inference semantics · checkpoint format · reference/optimized implementations.

BOUNDARY RULE
  “same name” or “same repository” ⊣ equality of 𝕀.

READING NOTES
1. θ is distinct from A: many checkpoints can instantiate one architecture.
2. Evaluation version matters because a corrected oracle can change a result without changing θ.
3. Hardware and precision enter claims about speed, memory, and numerical parity.
