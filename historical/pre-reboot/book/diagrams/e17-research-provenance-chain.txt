FAILURE RULE
  any missing identity ⊣ a fully reproducible claim; a name never repairs the missing link.

READING NOTES
1. Training and evaluation each depend on code, configuration, data, and environment.
2. A checkpoint alone does not identify the experiment that produced or measured it.
3. Corrections preserve the old chain and add a superseding chain with explicit linkage.
