## Pedagogical contract

Each candidate claim states its nearest prior art, formal difference, baseline, falsifier and evidence status. Experiments may be deferred only with a visible gate. Exercises include proofs, oracle construction, replication and architecture critique.

Appendices: mathematical prerequisites; biological terminology; reproducibility contracts; worked exercise solutions; glossary and index. Chapter count is subordinate to teaching quality.
